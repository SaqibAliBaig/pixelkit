Applying for a passport, visa, or government exam online usually comes with strict photo upload specifications: **exact pixel dimensions** (such as 413×531 px or 600×600 px) and a **maximum file size** (typically under 50KB or 100KB).

If your photo is off by a few pixels or a few kilobytes, the portal will immediately reject it with errors like *"Dimensions out of range"* or *"File exceeds size limit"*.

Here is how to get your passport photo right on the first try without uploading your personal face scan to any third-party servers.

---

## 1. Common Passport & Visa Dimension Standards

Different portals follow different international specifications:

* **Standard Passport (35 × 45 mm at 300 DPI)**: **413 × 531 pixels** (used by Indian passport portals, Schengen visas, and UK/EU official applications).
* **US Visa & Diversity Visa (2 × 2 inches at 300 DPI)**: **600 × 600 pixels** (1:1 square ratio).
* **Exam & Job Portals (UPSC, SSC, State Services)**: Usually **350 × 450 pixels** or between **20 KB and 50 KB**.

---

## 2. Step 1: Crop to the Correct Framing

Before resizing dimensions, crop the photo so your face and shoulders are properly centered:

1. Open [PixelKit Crop Studio](/crop-image) and drop your photo.
2. Select the **3:4** or **1:1** aspect ratio preset (depending on your country's requirements).
3. Align the visual rule-of-thirds grid so that your face covers roughly 70% to 80% of the frame.
4. Click **Apply Crop**.

---

## 3. Step 2: Set the Exact Pixel Dimensions

1. Click **Scale & Resize** on the file card.
2. Under **Standard Output Presets**, click **Passport ID (413 × 531 px)** or enter custom dimensions.
3. Ensure the aspect lock is checked so the photo doesn't stretch or distort.
4. Click **Apply Scale**.

---

## 4. Step 3: Compress Under 50KB or 100KB

Portals require photos to be lightweight for their database:

1. In the **Compression & Size** settings, select **Max Size** mode.
2. Choose **100 KB** (or **50 KB** if required by your application portal).
3. Set the output format to **JPG / JPEG** (universal format accepted by all portals).
4. Click **Convert & Compress Now**.

Your photo is processed completely in your browser memory and downloaded directly to your device, ready for acceptance on your first submission.
